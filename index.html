Questions clients ask

How does payment work?

Through Upwork or Fiverr. The platform holds the money and releases it when you accept the work. Bigger projects are split into milestones, so you pay part by part.

Can you work on a site I already have?

Yes. I work with WordPress, Laravel, React and plain HTML sites. Send me the link and what you want changed, and I will tell you the price and time.

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of your business, two or three sites you like, and your texts, photos and logo if you have them. If you do not, I can use placeholder content and you replace it later.

Do you set up hosting and the domain?

Yes. I help you choose a host and set everything up. The domain and hosting accounts stay in your name, so you own the site.

What happens after launch?

For 14 days I fix any bug for free. After that I can keep supporting the site for a small monthly fee, or you take it over.

Have a project in mind?

Tell me what you need in a few sentences. I reply within 12 hours with questions or a fixed quote.
