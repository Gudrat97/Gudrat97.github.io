    I deploy to your server or the app stores and fix anything you find in the first 14 days for free.

Questions clients ask

How does payment work?

Through Upwork or Fiverr. The platform holds the money and releases it when you accept each milestone, so you never pay for work you have not seen.

Can you work on an existing project?

Yes. I regularly take over Laravel, PHP, React and WordPress projects: fixing bugs, speeding them up or adding features. Send me the repository or a link and I will tell you the price and time.

Do I own the code?

Yes. The code lives in your repository from the first day, and the domain, server and app store accounts stay in your name.

Can you publish the app to the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. I prepare the builds, screenshots and store listings and handle the review process with your developer accounts.

What happens after launch?

For 14 days I fix any bug for free. After that I can keep supporting the product for a monthly fee, or hand it over with documentation.

Have a project in mind?

Tell me what you need in a few sentences. I reply within 12 hours with questions or a fixed quote.
